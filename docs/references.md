## Notes on three entries

**[16] is new and worth keeping.** Chen *et al.* (2022) documents the competence *gap itself* —
43 papers, finding that inadequate exposure and lack of regular training drive poor ECG competence
among nurses in acute care. Reference [1] shows that interventions help; [16] shows why they are
needed. Cite it in §2.2 alongside [1]. If your template enforces strict order-of-first-citation,
swap [16] into position [2] and renumber.

**[2] is the weakest entry.** I could not find it. Either locate the exact paper or remove it —
[1] and [16] carry §2.2 and §2.3 without it.

**[7]–[10] were not in the original project brief.** They are added because the implementation uses
them directly: Pan–Tompkins is the QRS detector, Bazett and Fridericia are the two QT corrections
reported, and the AHA/ACC/HRS recommendations define the measurement conventions and the
25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV display geometry. A methodology chapter describing that engine without citing
them would have a hole in it.
